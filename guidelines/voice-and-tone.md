# GRAM: Voice & Tone (Copywriting and Microcopy)

> **Status:** Draft v0.1, derived from the limited evidence in `brand.md` (profile bio, event poster, menu card text) and patterns in `competitor.md`. **All Slovak and English sample copy below is a proposal and must be reviewed by a native Slovak speaker and approved by the owners** before use.
> **Rule for agents:** don't invent facts (menu items, prices, awards, claims). Use placeholders like `{PRICE}` or `[TBD]` for unknowns.

---

## 1. Voice in one line
**Confident, relaxed and concise: a well-made espresso bar that doesn't take itself too seriously.**

## 2. Principles
| # | Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Short and direct** | Lines of 3–10 words. The existing bio is just name, descriptor, hours, address. Stay that economical. |
| 2 | **Plainspoken about coffee** | Say "výberová káva" / "specialty coffee" once, then describe by taste. No jargon walls, no snobbery. |
| 3 | **Relaxed, with a dry edge** | Light humor is on-brand, strongest in events (the menu card: "no rules, you're an adult"). Never sarcastic toward customers. |
| 4 | **Useful first** | Hours, address, price and menu come before personality. |
| 5 | **Honest** | Only claims we can back up. No "best coffee in Trnava". |
| 6 | **Bilingual, equal** | Slovak and English each read naturally, not as word-for-word translation. |

## 3. Language rules

**Slovak (primary)**
- Informal **"ty"** (as all three local competitors do): "pozri si menu", "nájdeš nás".
- Use real Slovak diacritics always.
- 24-hour time (7:30, 17:00); dates as `19. 9.` or `19. septembra`.
- Currency: `3,50 €` (comma decimals, euro sign after).

**English (secondary)**
- Simple and international; avoid idioms. 24-hour time to match the Slovak page (or "7:30 AM" consistently if the owners prefer).
- Spell "Štefánikova" with diacritics; it is a street name.

**Brand name**
- Always **GRAM** (uppercase). In running text use "GRAM" not "Gram"; the handle is `@gram.trnava`.
- Pair with place when context is thin: "GRAM espresso bar, Trnava".

**Uppercase style**
- Headings and labels display in uppercase via CSS; write them in normal case in the source.

**Mixed language**
- GRAM's social posts mix English headlines with Slovak details. On the website, **use full SK and EN versions**; a short English tagline may stay English on the Slovak page only if the owners confirm it's a brand line (e.g. the event tagline "Paired and perfectly balanced.").

## 4. Vocabulary
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| espresso bar, káva, výberová káva, matcha, ľadová káva | "artisanal", "curated experience", "gourmet", "elevated" |
| open, closed, today, tomorrow | marketing filler: "unique", "unforgettable", "world-class" |
| plain tasting words (sweet, creamy, bright, nutty) | long flavor-wheel lists |
| "Nájdeš nás", "Find us" | formal "Vás/Vy" in Slovak |
| numbers and facts | superlatives and unverified claims |

Avoid: emojis in UI copy (the profile uses none apart from a map pin); exclamation marks beyond one per page; mentioning competitors by name; swear words or heavy slang; claims about Wi-Fi, vegan options, roaster or origin until confirmed.

## 5. Tone by context
| Context | Tone | Length | Example direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero / marketing headers | Confident, minimal | 2–7 words | "Espresso bar v Trnave." |
| Section intros | Warm, factual | 1–2 sentences | Describe what's served and where |
| Menu items | Factual + one sensory word | ≤ 12 words | "Cold milk, espresso, light foam." |
| Event promos | Playful, "no rules" energy allowed | 1–3 sentences | Lead with date and time |
| Navigation and buttons | Neutral verbs | 1–2 words | "Menu", "Navigate" |
| Forms and system messages | Clear, calm | 1 sentence | Say what happened and what to do |
| Errors and 404 | Calm, a little wry | 1–2 sentences | Never blame the user |
| Legal / privacy | Plain, precise | As needed | No jokes |

### Image alt text patterns
- Describe the drink + setting in one sentence: "Iced matcha latte on a round oak table in sunlight." / SK equivalent.
- Don't start with "Image of…".

### Event copy pattern
1. Date and time first: "19. 9., 14:00–20:00".
2. One line on what happens (coffee, pizza, cocktails for the GRAM Dough collab).
3. Partner credit and link.
4. Optional wry line (e.g. "no rules, pair as you like"). Keep it short.

## 7. Good vs. bad
| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Experience the finest artisanal coffee journey in Trnava" | "Espresso bar v Trnave. Otvorené od 7:30." |
| "We are thrilled to announce an unforgettable event!!!" | "Piatok 19. 9., 14:00–20:00. Pizza, káva, drinky." |
| "Our baristas are the best in town" | "Káva na espresso aj ľadová. Pozri menu." |
| "Error 404: resource not found" | "Táto stránka tu nie je." |

## 8. Review checklist (for any new copy)
- [ ] Is every fact confirmed by the owners?
- [ ] Is it short enough? Could it lose a third of its words?
- [ ] Informal "ty" in Slovak, correct diacritics?
- [ ] Equivalent EN version?
- [ ] No unverified claims, no competitor names, no emoji?
- [ ] Practical info (hours, address) first?
