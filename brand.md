## 2. Visual identity

### Logo and wordmark [V]
- "GRAM" set in a heavy, wide, blocky geometric display face with chunky rounded-square letterforms and tight spacing.
- Primary use: **white on solid burgundy** (profile picture), or **burgundy on light grey** (posters).
- Also appears: white wordmark on white takeaway cups, and **engraved/embossed into a brushed-steel wall plate** (signage).

### Colors (approximated from screenshots, confirm hex with owner) [O]

| Role | Approx. value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#5A1A27` deep burgundy / wine | Logo, headlines, key info on posters |
| Background neutral | light warm grey `~#DEDADB` (with a torn-paper shape in slightly darker grey) | Poster backgrounds, highlight covers |
| Reverse | `#FFFFFF` | Logo on burgundy, cups |
| Material accents | natural light oak, brushed stainless steel, matte grey wall, black industrial stools | Interior and photo props |

Not GRAM brand colors: the royal-blue text box on the "3 cafés to visit" reel (looks like external creator styling) and the black/yellow of Punková Kuchyňa (partner).

### Typography [O]
Two-voice system:
1. **Display:** the heavy blocky wordmark face for "GRAM" (and collab names). Used in caps, large, burgundy.
2. **Secondary:** a clean, neutral geometric/grotesk sans.
   - Bold caps for sub-wordmarks (e.g. "DOUGH") and small info lists.
   - Light/regular weight for taglines ("Paired and perfectly balanced.") and times.
   - Bold numerals for dates ("19.9.").
- Small info text is set in bold uppercase, right-aligned, burgundy.

### Layout style [O]
- Flat, graphic, uncluttered. Big wordmark, torn-paper grey shape, plenty of empty space.
- Food or drink photography cropped into the poster layout (pizza bleeding off the top edge).
- Strict two-color feel (burgundy + grey/white) on designed graphics.

## 3. Photography and content direction [O]

**Look:** editorial, tactile, warm-meets-industrial.
- **Natural light**, often hard direct sun with strong shadows (outdoor shots on asphalt).
- **Recurring props/setting:** round light-oak stools used as small tables; brushed steel shelf/plate; grey wall; black stools; white ceramic cups; clear glassware.
- **Angles:** tight macro (espresso pouring, foam texture), low-angle looking up at a drink on a table, top-down on a tray of drinks with a hand entering frame.
- **Dark moody variant:** espresso shot in dramatic low-key light with lens flare/prismatic reflections.
- **Lifestyle:** shallow-depth street shot of a person (casual grey denim shirt, white tee) holding a white GRAM takeaway cup. Faces often cropped out or partial.
- **Color of food/drink as accent:** vivid matcha green, layered milk/espresso gradients, golden foam.

**Formats seen (first 8 posts):** 5 carousels, 2 reels, 1 single designed poster.
**Mix:** mostly product-led photography; designed graphics reserved for events and collabs.

## 6. Voice and language

### Language [O]
- **Bilingual, English + Slovak.** English is used for headlines, taglines and reel overlays ("Paired and perfectly balanced.", "SOFT OPENING WEEKEND", "ENJOY!"). Slovak is used for practical/product details ("PUNKOVÁ NEAPOLSKÁ PIZZA", "VÝBEROVÁ KÁVA", "SIGNATURE COCKTAILY").
- Slovak copy uses the informal **"ty"** form (e.g. "vyber si", "máš chuť").
- Official primary language of captions: **unconfirmed**. Ask owner.

### Tone [O, strongest on the collab content]
- Confident, relaxed, a bit irreverent. Short lines, no corporate polish.
- Playful "no rules" attitude. Sample (menu card, rotated and partly legible, so check against original): *"Párovanie odporúčame, alebo si vyber samostatne na čo máš chuť. Pravidlá nemáme. „Rob jak sceš, si dospelý." Enjoy!"* (Gloss: "We recommend pairing, or pick whatever you feel like. We have no rules. 'Do what you want, you're an adult.' Enjoy!")
- Bio and profile copy remain minimal and factual, so the cheekiness may partly come from the punk-themed partner. Don't overdo it in everyday GRAM content without confirmation.
- Style: uppercase for labels and headings, minimal emoji (a flag emoji appears only on the third-party reel), no hashtag use observed.

## 10. Guidance for agents

1. **Never fabricate** menu items, prices, origin stories, or quotes. Use only verified/observed facts, or flag assumptions.
2. **Name:** "GRAM" in caps. Handle `@gram.trnava`. Collab naming: "Punková Kuchyňa × GRAM Dough".
3. **Designed graphics:** burgundy + light grey/white, giant blocky GRAM wordmark, clean bold sans for secondary text, torn-paper shape, generous whitespace.
4. **Photos/AI image briefs:** natural hard light, light-oak stool props, steel and grey surfaces, white cups and clear glass, tight crops, low or top-down angles. Avoid rustic, cozy-vintage, or heavily filtered looks.
5. **Copy:** short, relaxed, English headline + Slovak details; informal "ty" in Slovak. Keep cheeky "no rules" energy mainly for events and collabs.
6. **Practical info** (address, hours) must be checked against the live Instagram bio before publishing.
7. **Don't use** partner assets (duck mascot, Punková Kuchyňa logo) or third-party styling (blue overlay) as GRAM brand elements.
8. **When unsure,** ask the owner or requesting user rather than filling gaps.
